     ranges 0.309-0.658 (mean 0.492); rotated arms 0.459-0.545 (mean 0.499).
 F4. sign(C_ij / sigma_j) == sign(C_ij) exactly: 0 of 63,552 doc bits and 0 of 8,160 query bits
