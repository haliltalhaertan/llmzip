## 2. Directions you missed (where "that's the real result" lives)

1. **Tie-geometry theory of sign retrieval (highest scientific upside).** The separation paradox + R2A (all \|r\|≤0.15 for N/gold-count/rank/tie-cluster/dup-fraction; ties dominate 250/470; hard losers: TOP48 rank 10.8/cluster 6.1 vs RAND 0.6/1.3 on the decisive subset) says: with 96-bit Hamming codes, top-3 is decided by exact-tie mass and near-boundary competition, not mean distance. The paper-worthy claim is not "random beats variance" but "**mean-separation statistics do not order sign-code retrieval quality; tie-mass at the top-3 boundary does.**" Cheap tests: per-question tie-mass decomposition at the top-3 boundary (mass at d_gold, mass at d_gold±1, cluster size of gold's bucket, margin to 4th doc); duplicate-code bucket analysis extended beyond "not explained by collapse alone"; Hamming-distance histogram overlap metrics. If a 2–3-feature tie model predicts flip direction out-of-sample while mean stats cannot, that is the reviewer's "real result."
2. **Why does centered SIGN96 beat float96 by +10 pp at all?** The programme's founding anomaly is under-theorized. Candidates: hubness reduction via binarization, centering removing a dominant common-mode, tie-breaking interacting with float near-ties, query-archive asymmetry. A cheap, gold-free probe panel (hubness k-occurrence skew float vs sign; common-mode ablation; float near-tie rate vs sign tie rate; centered vs uncentered sign gap) belongs before any new codec comparison — if the sign advantage is a centering/hubness artifact, every (b) arm inherits it and RaBitQ/PQ comparisons must match centering exactly.
3. **Archive-local heterogeneity: variance ranking is per-archive, utility is global.** All utilities so far are global axis sets applied to per-archive variance structures. Stratify by archive size, archive variance-decay slope, gold cardinality, question type (single-session-user gap −0.30 pp is 5× multi-session −0.06 — the most type-concentrated effect in R2A, currently unexploited). Test: does drop-utility transfer across archives, or is there an archive-cluster × axis interaction? A per-archive adaptive subset (still gold-free at eval: pick subset from archive stats only) is NOES-safer than per-question adaptation and could rescue part of the 6B −9.2 pp gap.
4. **Per-axis reliability, not per-axis variance.** Every axis has delta ≥0.066 (all 96 weakly positive), no axis load-bearing (30/96 drop-loss ≤0), alone-FR hopeless (0.006–0.010 raw; 0.55–0.80 pool-normalized). The right abstraction may be *bit reliability* P(sign agrees with query ∣ gold) estimated gold-free from archive-query agreement rates, not variance. A reliability-ordered subset vs variance-ordered vs random contrast is a cleaner scientific bet than hetero-precision, and its failure would be informative (it would say joint tie structure dominates marginal reliability too).
5. **Publish the nulls as results.** ITQ-family below 96 bits FAILED; variance-order loses ~10 pp vs random with W/T/L 91/179/200; variance control worst at every k out-of-sample; mixing damage strictly ordered by disparity. In a field that rewards positive codec claims, a preregistered, independently-recomputed set of *signed nulls with tolerance bands* ("top-variance selection is worse than random by ≥X pp on both benchmarks") is more credible than another +1.6 pp premium hunt. Frame (b) to include one-sided non-inferiority/superiority bands for the "never do this" arms too.

## 3. Sharpest CHEAP designs for the top 3 (arms, controls, gates, kill criteria)

All three reuse the frozen T4C3/T4D pipeline verbatim, frozen anchors as abort gates, 20-trial tie protocol, fractional R@3, per-question persistence. All are minutes-scale. None touches BEAM/4F1.

### Design 1 — (a) Learned-premium robustness (the single most important next experiment)

- **Arms (test side, LME first, then LoCoMo mirror):** drop64, alone64 (the two errata-corrected winners), drop48 (borderline +0.88 vs mean), var64/var48 (negative controls, must stay worst), SPREAD (true rank-linspace, fixed construction — the stride-2 cap artifact must be repaired so SPREAD64≠SPREAD48), RANDOM-k with **10 fresh seeds** (not 3; the 3.34 pp range at k=64 demands it), NATIVE96 reference. k ∈ {32,48,64} minimum.
- **Controls:** 10 fresh train/test splits (qid-sha256 parity was one arbitrary split with KU skew 43/29 and harder test half — rotate the hash salt / use 10-fold question splits, stratified by question_type); utilities computed **train-only** (provenance check: recompute train top-5 must match logged cols exactly, full-data ranking must differ — the R2D discrimination test); identical tie seeds; per-split W/T/L vs best-seed (not vs mean — headline must be vs-best, vs-mean reported second).
- **Gates (preregister as aborts):** native recompute vs frozen anchor ≤1e-12 per-question max; split disjointness + coverage; utility provenance exact-match; SPREAD construction assert (eff_k==k, cols logged, no sentinel).
- **Primary estimand (freeze before running):** mean over splits of (drop64_test − best-RANDOM64_test_on_that_split), with per-split values + question-paired bootstrap CI. Same for alone64. Report vs-mean secondarily.
- **Kill/promote:** **KILL learned selection from (b)** if mean-vs-best < +1.0 pp (the 1.0 pp band) *or* win-rate vs best < 7/10 splits *or* LoCoMo mirror mean-vs-best ≤ 0. **PROMOTE to (b)** if mean-vs-best ≥ +1.5 pp *and* win-rate ≥ 8/10 *and* (e1) transfer (below) is non-negative. Between (+1.0–1.5, 7/10) → carry as a *secondary* (b) arm, not primary. Report drop48/alone64 honestly even if they die — the variance-control-must-lose check (var worst at every k on every split) is a validity gate: if var ever wins, the pipeline, not the theory, is suspect.
- **Cost:** ~10 splits × ~20 arms × frozen eval = minutes; the value is entirely in killing a fragile arm before it enters a seal.

### Design 2 — (b) Twelve-byte budget race (preregistration revision, then one run)

- **Budget definition (already decided, freeze verbatim):** ≤12 **marginal persistent bytes/vector** (code + any norm/scale/correction scalar/per-vector metadata); shared state (codebooks, OPQ rotation, centroid tables) outside the cap but **always reported in bytes separately**; declare archive-local fitting; cross-archive/global codebooks are a different deployment config.
- **Arms (each asserting `measured_bytes == declared` pre-compute, abort otherwise):** NATIVE96 SIGN (12B reference); spread/random subsets at 10B/8B/6B (true constructions, ≥5 seeds each — the pilots' "which bits" baseline; BOT-tail included on LoCoMo given BOT80 −0.1 pp); learned-64 **only if Design 1 promotes it** (else spread/random takes its slot — do not seal both variants, seal the gating rule); RABITQ32 plain + RABITQ32 with random-rotation wrapper (the two RQ namings; verify Faiss `IndexRaBitQ` rotation chain, do not assume wrapper); PQ m=12×8bit (fits marginal cap; report per-archive effective cost on small archives, not hidden); extended-RaBitQ (44B) **excluded** from the matched-budget contrast (report on a recall-vs-actual-bytes curve only). Both benchmarks, same tie scheme.
- **Controls:** native reproduction vs both frozen anchors; `code_size` abort (catches the silent `nb_bits` trap); rotation/orthogonality/invariance checks carried from the validated runners; per-question records per arm/seed; negative controls proving each check can fail; pinned third-party package+version installed-not-vendored (per the install-not-vendor policy; one package suffices since faiss-cpu supplies both RaBitQ and PQ).
- **Primary estimand:** per-seed SIGN12B − best-competitor-12B difference, seed-panel mean + per-seed range, question-paired bootstrap (both benchmarks) + conversation-cluster bootstrap (LoCoMo only), reported separately per benchmark (no pooling). Bands frozen in advance on the programme's 0.5/1.0/2.0 pp scale (e.g. ≥+2.0 pp decisive, +1.0–2.0pp moderate, ±0.5pp parity, ≤−1.0pp against).
- **Kill/promote:** **KILL the "SIGN96 competitive at 12B" line** if SIGN12B loses to any 12B-total competitor by ≥2.0 pp on either benchmark (or if the recall-vs-bytes curve shows SIGN dominated everywhere). **PROMOTE to engineering** if SIGN12B wins/parities (≥−0.5 pp) on both benchmarks with per-seed ranges non-overlapping against the "alternate-widths fail" null. If results split by benchmark (as the pilots' bottom-vs-spread nuance predicts), report heterogeneity — do not average it away.
- **What makes this preregistrable now:** the pilots to disclose (axis-attack r1+r2 as exploratory source for the spread/random arms + learned arm if included), the byte-cost replay evidence (20/44/12 measurements, 8B fixed overhead, d=32 max), the budget-decision artifact, seed panels declared as literals, third-party pins.

### Design 3 — (c1) Tie-mass decomposition + gold-free failure predictor (mechanism, two stages)

- **Stage c1 (descriptive, must precede c2):** per-question, per-arm (TOP48, RAND48, BOT48, drop64): tie-mass at d_gold, at d_gold±1, gold-bucket cluster size, margin (d_4th − d_gold), duplicate-bucket size, top-20 distance entropy, strictly-closer count. Test the paradox directly: does a 2–3-feature tie model (logistic, fitted on train split only) predict TOP-vs-RAND flip direction on held-out questions while mean stats (gold distance, min non-gold, crowding, mean rank) cannot? Report AUC for tie-model vs mean-model + ablation. **Kill c2 if tie-model AUC < 0.65 or tie−mean ΔAUC < 0.05** — the story stays "descriptive paradox," no predictor is claimed.
- **Stage c2 (predictor → adaptive, exploratory):** gold-free features only (no gold distance — use margin-to-top3-boundary, cluster size at boundary, duplicate fraction, archive variance-decay slope, N_archive; all computable without gold). Predict small-budget failure (e.g. 6B subset fails where 12B succeeds). Evaluate as selective prediction: abstain/route-to-larger-budget on predicted-fail questions at fixed abstention rates; report gain vs random-abstain baseline. **Promote to preregistered adaptive experiment only if** selective gain ≥ +1.0 pp at 20% abstention on held-out splits *and* replicates on LoCoMo. Default expectation (state in advance): good AUC, no cheap adaptive gain — that null is itself the mechanism result (failures are identifiable but not avoidably structured).
- **Controls/gates:** features frozen before fitting; train/test split hygiene as in Design 1 (gold-informed tie features in c1 tagged analysis-only; c2 features provably gold-free by construction audit); multi-seed baselines for every W/T/L claim (R2A's single-seed RAND48_s0 + 3.5 pp dispersion must not recur).

## 5. Risks & falsifiers

**What would falsify the pilot findings (name them so the programme can be proven wrong):**

1. Top-variance anti-optimality dies if TOP-k beats random-mean on a fresh split/seed panel at any k≥32 on either benchmark, or if var-utility is not worst at every k in Design 1's 10 splits. Prior: unlikely (replicated out-of-sample + cross-benchmark), but the LoCoMo k=16 inversion (TOP beats RANDOM at k16) already bounds the claim — it is a k≥32 claim, not universal.
2. Mixing-disparity ordering dies if matched ≈ random ≈ anti on fresh Q blocks (the current pairing confounds grouping with Q-to-axis assignment; R2D flagged it). If fresh-Q matched still gives −0.04 pp on LoCoMo with strict separation, the ordering graduates from pilot to finding.
3. Learned premium dies if Design 1's mean-vs-best < +1.0 pp or win-rate < 7/10 — my modal expectation is ~40% it dies, ~40% it lands in the ambiguous +1.0–1.5 zone, ~20% it promotes cleanly. The single-split +1.62 vs best with 3.34 pp seed noise should never have been headlined without this test.
4. "Bottom-tail best on LoCoMo vs spread/random best on LME" dies if it collapses under more seeds (both tails are single-construction arms; RANDOM ranges already overlap them at several k). Treat benchmark-dependence of *where good bits live* as the fragile half of R2C, and the *top-is-worst + disparity-ordering* half as the robust core.
5. Tie-story dies if mean stats predict flips as well as tie features in (c1), or if the failure predictor's AUC ≈ 0.5 gold-free. Then the paradox stays a curiosity and adaptivity is abandoned.

**Failure modes of this plan:**

- **Seed-noise laundering:** any headline vs random-mean without vs-best + range repeats R2D F1/F3/F12. Fix by freezing vs-best as primary everywhere.
- **SPREAD-style construction bugs:** stride/sentinel/cap errors already occurred twice. Fix with `eff_k==k` asserts + logged cols + pre-compute aborts (same discipline as the `code_size` abort).
- **Test-half hardness + split skew:** R2B's test was harder (0.519 vs 0.564) with KU skew. Fix with stratified multi-split + per-split best-seed comparators (absolute levels will still vary; only within-split gaps are interpreted).
- **Benchmark-averaging:** LME and LoCoMo disagree on where good bits live. Pooling them would manufacture a false consensus. Report separately, always.
- **Premature hetero/adaptive engineering:** both are NOES-adjacent and would burn the preregistration budget before (b) answers the programme's actual question. Gate them behind Designs 1/c1.

**NOES / 4F1 avoidance (explicit):**

- Do not run whitening, PCA/learned rotations, variance reweighting (beyond frozen subset selection), learned thresholds, reranking, supervised rotation, or alternate metrics without a new preregistration naming them. The pilots already approach (but do not cross, with disclosure) alternate-widths, variance selection/matching, supervised-selection analogue, extra seeds — every future preregistration must list these four as approached-with-disclosure.
- Do not touch BEAM data, `--mode run/finalize`, `run_archives`/`evaluate_archive`/`finalize_results`, production auth keys, or any retrieval-quality outcome on 4F1. The twelve-byte and mechanism work uses only the frozen LME/LoCoMo pipelines and certified matrices. No 4F1 seed, runner, or corpus read is authorized by this note.
