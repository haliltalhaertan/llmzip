magnitude quoted in one sentence was wrong.

**Does not reopen the audit.** Its verdict remains as recorded.

SEALED / RUN BLOCKED / OUTCOME ACCESS FORBIDDEN.

## Why it is worth recording

The programme's own discipline is that a quoted number is an assertion and must be
reproducible from bytes. `231,606` is reproducible only if `__SUMMARY__` rows are read
as archives. A later reader taking `N_archive ≈ 231,606` at face value would
mis-scale every argument that depends on archive size — including the per-archive fit
argument, where 231,606 rows and 496 rows imply very different things.
