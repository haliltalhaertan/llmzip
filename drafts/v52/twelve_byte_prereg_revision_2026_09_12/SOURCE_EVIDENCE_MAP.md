published scalar report, protocol text, Git metadata, and its own new draft files.

JSONs outside the supplied published storage evidence, or HMAC material. No
Python/Faiss script, control, trial, corpus evaluation, or prior row-level result
recomputation is performed. The numerical work is limited to the five published
SIMHASH scalar percentages and byte/hash verification. There is no web or external
model research, no G3 change, and no change to the original checkout.
