## The three questions

**1. Is the remedy sound?** Yes, with framing as the load-bearing condition. At "12 bytes total including all per-vector auxiliary data," `d=32` is not a choice but the *only* 12-byte 1-bit RaBitQ (C5), so no fairer RaBitQ configuration is being withheld. The "96 dims vs 32 dims" objection misidentifies the estimand: the prereg asks about retrieval per stored byte, not estimator quality per dimension — format efficiency (bits/dim plus aux overhead) is part of what is being compared, and SIGN96's 1-bit/dim with zero aux *is* its format advantage. A reviewer rejects this only if it is sold as method-vs-method at fixed dims; sold as format-vs-format at fixed bytes, with the recall-versus-actual-bytes curve as the primary figure (it shows whether any SIGN96 edge survives across operating points instead of at one point), it is defensible. Dropping arm 5 from the matched contrast is mandatory (44 B); keep it descriptive or drop it. Keep each arm's published query-side estimator (§6 lines 66–69) — "fixing" SIGN96 with asymmetric scoring would break the frozen §8 controls.

**2. Per-vector or amortised?** Per-vector marginal persistent bytes (the L-088 rule, confirmed at `main:docs/CONTINUITY_LEDGER.md` line 1873) is correct: shared state amortises to ~0 at archive scale, and folding it in would let codebook methods hide capacity behind small test archives. Under L-088 no other arm is secretly over budget per-vector — SIGN96/ITQ96/SIMHASH96/RANDOM96 store 12 B of bits; OPQ_PQ96 stores 12 B of codes (`m=12 × 8 bit`); TRUNC12 stores 12 int8s. But arm 6's shared state is large and must be tabulated, not footnoted: OPQ rotation 96×96 float32 = 36,864 B plus PQ centroids 12×256×8 float32 = 98,304 B (~135 KB/archive, plus ITQ/SIMHASH rotations likewise reported). On small archives that dwarfs the per-vector saving — L-088 already orders archive-local accounting, and the revision should show a per-archive shared-state table or a reviewer will (rightly) ask where the codebooks went. Genuinely unexamined by both sessions: query-side cost (asymmetric-lookup compute; arm 7 keeps a float query) and index-structure overhead beyond codes (IDs, lists). The revision should state the cap covers stored bytes per vector only, with query compute explicitly out of scope — otherwise a reviewer moves the goalposts later.

**3. A cheaper experiment?** The cheapest decisive step is already half done: this byte audit (arms 4/5 were never at budget — no retrieval needed to void the contrast). For signal: a single-panel pilot — LongMemEval only, four truly-12B arms (SIGN96, RABITQ32, OPQ_PQ96, TRUNC12_INT8) plus the 384 B ceiling as reference, reusing the frozen pipeline that §8 controls already validate, with 5 seeds only where stochastic (OPQ/PQ; RaBitQ/SIGN/TRUNC are deterministic given data). If SIGN96 does not beat RABITQ32/OPQ_PQ96 on the home panel, a B1/B2 outcome in the full run is already unlikely and the programme learns it in about a day. Limit: a pilot cannot trigger decision bands (they need both benchmarks) — it is a gate to the full run, not a substitute.
